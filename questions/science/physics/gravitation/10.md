**If the mass and radius of the Earth are both doubled, then the acceleration due to gravity g' is**

```markdown
Acceleration due to gravity g = GM/R². If M becomes 2M and R becomes 2R, then g' = G(2M)/(2R)² = 2GM/4R² = (1/2)GM/R² = g/2.
```
