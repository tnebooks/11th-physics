answers:
  - decreases in the month of July and increases in the month of January

**The magnitude of the Sun's gravitational field as experienced by Earth is**

```markdown
Earth is closest to the Sun in January (perihelion) and farthest in July (aphelion). Gravitational field E = GM/r². So it is maximum in January (when r is minimum) and minimum in July (when r is maximum). Therefore, it decreases in July and increases in January.
```
