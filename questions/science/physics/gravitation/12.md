**If a person moves from Chennai to Trichy, his weight**

```markdown
Chennai is at sea level while Trichy is at a higher altitude. As we move to higher altitudes, the distance from the centre of the Earth increases, so acceleration due to gravity decreases. Therefore, weight decreases.
```
