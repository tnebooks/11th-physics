**An object of mass 10 kg is hanging on a spring scale which is attached to the roof of a lift. If the lift is in free fall, the reading in the spring scale is**

```markdown
During free fall, the acceleration of the lift is equal to g downward. The apparent weight of the object becomes zero because the spring scale and the object both fall with the same acceleration. So the reading is zero.
```
