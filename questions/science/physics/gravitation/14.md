**If the acceleration due to gravity becomes 4 times its original value, then escape speed**

```markdown
Escape speed vₑ = √(2gR). If g becomes 4g, then vₑ' = √(2(4g)R) = √4 × √(2gR) = 2vₑ. So escape speed becomes 2 times the original value.
```
