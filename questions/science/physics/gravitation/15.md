**The kinetic energy of the satellite orbiting around the Earth is**

```markdown
For a satellite in circular orbit, kinetic energy K = GMm/2r and potential energy U = -GMm/r. Therefore, K = -U/2. So the kinetic energy is less than the magnitude of potential energy.
```
