**If the masses of the Earth and Sun suddenly double, the gravitational force between them will**

```markdown
Gravitational force F = GMm/r². If both masses double, then F' = G(2M)(2m)/r² = 4GMm/r² = 4F. Therefore, the force increases by 4 times.
```
