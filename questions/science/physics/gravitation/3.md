**A planet moving along an elliptical orbit is closest to the Sun at distance r₁ and farthest away at a distance of r₂. If v₁ and v₂ are linear speeds at these points respectively. Then the ratio v₁/v₂ is**

```markdown
By conservation of angular momentum, mvr = constant. So v₁r₁ = v₂r₂. Therefore, v₁/v₂ = r₂/r₁.
```
