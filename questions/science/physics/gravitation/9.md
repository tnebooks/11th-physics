**The work done by the Sun's gravitational force on the Earth is**

```markdown
The work done by the Sun's gravitational force on Earth can be positive (when Earth moves towards the Sun), negative (when Earth moves away from the Sun), or zero (at perihelion and aphelion). So it can be positive or negative.
```
