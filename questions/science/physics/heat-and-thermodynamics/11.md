**A distant star emits radiation with maximum intensity at 350 nm. The temperature of the star is**

```markdown
Using Wien's displacement law: λ_max T = b, where b = 2.898 × 10⁻³ m K. λ_max = 350 nm = 350 × 10⁻⁹ m. T = b/λ_max = (2.898 × 10⁻³)/(350 × 10⁻⁹) = 8280 K.
```
