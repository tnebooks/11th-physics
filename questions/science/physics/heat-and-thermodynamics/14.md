**The efficiency of a heat engine working between the freezing point and boiling point of water is**

```markdown
Freezing point of water = 0°C = 273 K. Boiling point of water = 100°C = 373 K. Efficiency η = 1 - T_L/T_H = 1 - 273/373 = 100/373 ≈ 0.268 = 26.8%.
```
