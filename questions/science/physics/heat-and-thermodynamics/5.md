**When a uniform rod is heated, which of the following quantity of the rod will increase**

```markdown
When a rod is heated, its dimensions increase. Mass and weight remain unchanged as the amount of matter is the same. Centre of mass remains at the centre. Moment of inertia I = MR² increases because the distance of mass elements from the axis increases.
```
