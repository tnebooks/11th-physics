**When you exercise in the morning, by considering your body as thermodynamic system, which of the following is true?**

```markdown
During exercise, the body does work (W > 0) and loses internal energy (ΔU < 0) as heat is released to maintain body temperature. So ΔU < 0 and W > 0.
```
