**An ideal gas is taken from (P, V) to (P, V_f) in three different ways. Identify the process in which the work done on the gas the most.**

![](img.png)

```markdown
Work done on the gas is the area under the PV curve. The process with the largest area under the curve has the maximum work done. From the graph, Process B has the maximum area under the curve, so work done on the gas is most in Process B.
```
