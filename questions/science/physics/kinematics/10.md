**A ball is dropped from some height towards the ground. Which one of the following represents the correct motion of the ball?**

```markdown
When a ball is dropped from a height, it accelerates downward due to gravity. Its velocity increases linearly with time and its displacement increases as t². The correct motion diagram would show increasing spacing between successive positions.
```
