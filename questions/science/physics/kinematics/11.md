**If a particle executes uniform circular motion in the xy plane in clockwise direction, then the angular velocity is in**

```markdown
The direction of angular velocity is given by the right-hand rule. For clockwise motion in the xy plane, the angular velocity vector points in the -z direction (into the plane).
```
