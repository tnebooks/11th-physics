**If a particle executes uniform circular motion, choose the correct statement (NEET 2016)**

```markdown
In uniform circular motion, the speed (magnitude of velocity) is constant and the magnitude of acceleration (centripetal acceleration) is constant. However, velocity and acceleration are vectors whose directions change continuously.
```
