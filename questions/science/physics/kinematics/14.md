**Two objects are projected at angles 30° and 60° respectively with respect to the horizontal direction. The range of two objects are denoted as R₃₀ and R₆₀. Choose the correct relation from the following**

```markdown
The range of a projectile is given by R = u²sin2θ/g. For θ = 30°, sin60° = √3/2. For θ = 60°, sin120° = √3/2. Since sin2θ is the same for complementary angles (30° and 60°), the ranges are equal. Therefore, R₃₀ = R₆₀.
```
