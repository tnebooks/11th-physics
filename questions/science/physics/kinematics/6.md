**If the velocity is $\vec{v} = 2 \hat{i} + t^2 \hat{j} - 9 \hat{k}$, then the magnitude of acceleration at $t = 0.5$ s is**

```markdown
Acceleration $\vec{a} = d\vec{v}/dt = 0\hat{i} + 2t\hat{j} + 0\hat{k} = 2t\hat{j}$. At t = 0.5 s, $\vec{a} = 2(0.5)\hat{j} = 1\hat{j}$. Magnitude of acceleration = 1 m/s².
```
