**If an object is dropped from the top of a building and it reaches the ground at $t = 4 \, \text{s}$, then the height of the building is (ignoring air resistance) (g = 9.8 m/s²)**

```markdown
Using the equation of motion: s = ut + ½gt². Here u = 0 (dropped from rest), t = 4 s, g = 9.8 m/s². s = 0 + ½ × 9.8 × 4² = ½ × 9.8 × 16 = 9.8 × 8 = 78.4 m.
```
