**A ball is projected vertically upwards with a velocity v. It comes back to ground in time t. Which v-t graph shows the motion correctly? (NSEP 00-01)**

```markdown
When a ball is projected vertically upwards, its velocity decreases linearly to zero at the highest point, then becomes negative (downward) and increases in magnitude until it returns to the ground. The v-t graph is a straight line with negative slope crossing the time axis at the highest point.
```
