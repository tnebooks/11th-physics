**A particle of mass m is moving with speed u in a direction which makes 60° with respect to x axis. It undergoes elastic collision with the wall. What is the change in momentum in x and y direction?**

![](img.png)

```markdown
Initial momentum: p_xi = mu cos60° = mu/2, p_yi = mu sin60° = √3mu/2. After elastic collision with a wall (vertical wall), only the x-component changes sign: p_xf = -mu/2, p_yf = √3mu/2. Change in momentum: Δp_x = -mu/2 - mu/2 = -mu, Δp_y = 0.
```
