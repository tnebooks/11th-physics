**Which of the following shows the correct relationship between the pressure and density of an ideal gas at constant temperature?**

![](img1.png)

```markdown
At constant temperature, P = ρRT/M. So P is directly proportional to density ρ. The graph between P and ρ should be a straight line passing through the origin. Option (d) shows this correct relationship.
```
