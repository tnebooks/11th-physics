**A sample of gas consists of μ₁ moles of monoatomic molecules, μ₂ moles of diatomic molecules and μ₃ moles of linear triatomic molecules. The gas is kept at high temperature. What is the total number of degrees of freedom?**

```markdown
Monoatomic molecules have 3 translational degrees of freedom (total = 3). Diatomic molecules have 3 translational + 2 rotational + 2 vibrational = 7 degrees of freedom at high temperature. Linear triatomic molecules also have 3 translational + 2 rotational + 2 vibrational = 7 degrees of freedom. Total = [3μ₁ + 7(μ₂ + μ₃)]N_A.
```
