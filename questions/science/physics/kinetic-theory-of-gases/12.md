choices:
  - $s_p - s_v = 28R$
  - $s_p - s_v = R/14$
  - $s_p - s_v = R$
answers:
  - $s_p - s_v = R/28$
---

**If s_p and s_v denote the specific heats of nitrogen gas per unit mass at constant pressure and constant volume respectively, then**

```markdown
For nitrogen (N₂), molar mass M = 28 g/mol = 28 × 10⁻³ kg/mol. Specific heats per unit mass: s_p = Cp/M and s_v = Cv/M. s_p - s_v = (Cp - Cv)/M = R/M. Here R is universal gas constant per mole, so R/M = R/28.
```
