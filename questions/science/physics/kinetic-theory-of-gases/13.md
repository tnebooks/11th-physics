**Which of the following gases will have least rms speed at a given temperature?**

```markdown
RMS speed v_rms = √(3RT/M). At a given temperature, v_rms ∝ 1/√M. The gas with the largest molar mass will have the least rms speed. Among the options, CO₂ has the largest molar mass (44 g/mol), so it will have the least rms speed.
```
