**For a given gas molecule at a fixed temperature, the area under the Maxwell-Boltzmann distribution curve is equal to**

```markdown
The area under the Maxwell-Boltzmann distribution curve represents the total number of molecules in the system, which is N = PV/(kT) from the ideal gas equation.
```
