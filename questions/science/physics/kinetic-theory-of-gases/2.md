**A sample of ideal gas is at equilibrium. Which of the following quantity is zero?**

```markdown
At equilibrium, gas molecules move in random directions with equal probability in all directions. The vector sum of velocities of all molecules is zero, so the average velocity is zero. RMS speed, average speed, and most probable speed are non-zero.
```
