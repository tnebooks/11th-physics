**An ideal gas is maintained at constant pressure. If the temperature of an ideal gas increases from 100K to 10000K then the rms speed of the gas molecules**

```markdown
RMS speed v_rms ∝ √T. v₂/v₁ = √(T₂/T₁) = √(10000/100) = √100 = 10. So the rms speed increases by 10 times.
```
