**Two identically sized rooms A and B are connected by an open door. If the room A is air conditioned such that its temperature is 4°C lesser than room B, which room has more air in it?**

```markdown
At constant pressure, density ρ ∝ 1/T. Room A has lower temperature, so it has higher density. For the same volume, the room with higher density has more air molecules. Therefore, Room A has more air.
```
