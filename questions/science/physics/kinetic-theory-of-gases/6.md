**If the internal energy of an ideal gas U and volume V are doubled then the pressure**

```markdown
For an ideal gas, U = nCvT. If U doubles, T doubles (since n and Cv are constant). From ideal gas equation P = nRT/V. If T doubles and V doubles, P = nR(2T)/(2V) = nRT/V, so pressure remains the same.
```
