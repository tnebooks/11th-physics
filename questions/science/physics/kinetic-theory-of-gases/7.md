**The ratio γ = Cp/Cv for a gas mixture consisting of 8 g of helium and 16 g of oxygen is**

```markdown
Helium (monoatomic): n₁ = 8/4 = 2 moles, Cv₁ = 3R/2, Cp₁ = 5R/2. Oxygen (diatomic): n₂ = 16/32 = 0.5 moles, Cv₂ = 5R/2, Cp₂ = 7R/2. Total Cv = 2(3R/2) + 0.5(5R/2) = 3R + 1.25R = 4.25R = 17R/4. Total Cp = 2(5R/2) + 0.5(7R/2) = 5R + 1.75R = 6.75R = 27R/4. γ = Cp/Cv = (27R/4)/(17R/4) = 27/17.
```
