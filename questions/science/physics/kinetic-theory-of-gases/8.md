**A container has one mole of monoatomic ideal gas. Each molecule has f degrees of freedom. What is the ratio of γ = Cp/Cv?**

```markdown
For a gas with f degrees of freedom, Cv = fR/2 and Cp = Cv + R = (f/2 + 1)R = (f+2)R/2. Therefore, γ = Cp/Cv = [(f+2)R/2]/[fR/2] = (f+2)/f.
```
