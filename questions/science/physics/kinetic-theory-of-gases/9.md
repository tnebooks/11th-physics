**If the temperature and pressure of a gas is doubled the mean free path of the gas molecules**

```markdown
Mean free path λ = kT/(√2πd²P). If T and P both double, then λ' = k(2T)/(√2πd²(2P)) = kT/(√2πd²P) = λ. So the mean free path remains the same.
```
