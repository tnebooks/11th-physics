**Choose the correct statement from the following**

```markdown
Centripetal force is the real force that acts towards the centre in circular motion. Centrifugal force is a pseudo force that appears to act away from the centre in a rotating frame. They are not action-reaction pairs. Centripetal force is not a natural force by itself; it is provided by some real force. Centrifugal force does not arise from gravitational force.
```
