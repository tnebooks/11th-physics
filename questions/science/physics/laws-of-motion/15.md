**If a person moving from pole to equator, the centrifugal force acting on him**

```markdown
Centrifugal force is given by F = mω²r, where r is the distance from the axis of rotation. At the pole, r = 0, so centrifugal force is zero. As a person moves from pole to equator, r increases, so the centrifugal force increases.
```
