**An object of mass m held against a vertical wall by applying horizontal force F as shown in the figure. The minimum value of the force F is (IIT JEE 1994)**

```markdown
For the object to be held against the wall, the frictional force must balance the weight mg. Friction f = μs F, where F is the applied horizontal force. For equilibrium, μs F ≥ mg, which gives F ≥ mg/μs. Since μs is always less than 1, F must be greater than mg.
```
