**A book is at rest on the table which exerts a normal force on the book. If this force is considered as reaction force, what is the action force according to Newton's third law?**

```markdown
According to Newton's third law, action and reaction forces act on different bodies. The normal force exerted by the table on the book is the reaction force. The action force is the normal force exerted by the book on the table.
```
