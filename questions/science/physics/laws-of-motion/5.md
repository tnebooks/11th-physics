**Two masses m₁ and m₂ are experiencing the same force where m₁ < m₂. The ratio of their acceleration a₁/a₂ is**

```markdown
According to Newton's second law, F = ma. Since both masses experience the same force, a₁ = F/m₁ and a₂ = F/m₂. Therefore, a₁/a₂ = (F/m₁)/(F/m₂) = m₂/m₁. Since m₁ < m₂, m₂/m₁ > 1. So the ratio is greater than 1.
```
