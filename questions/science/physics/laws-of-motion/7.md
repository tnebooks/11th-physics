**A particle of mass m sliding on the smooth double inclined plane (shown in figure) will experience**

```markdown
On a smooth inclined plane, acceleration is given by a = g sin θ. For path AB, the angle is 45° so a = g sin 45° = g/√2. For path AC, the angle is 30° so a = g sin 30° = g/2. Since g/√2 > g/2, the acceleration is greater along path AB.
```
