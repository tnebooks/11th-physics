**The centre of mass of a system of particles does not depend upon, (AIPMT 1997, AIEEE 2004)**

```markdown
The centre of mass depends on the positions, relative distances, and masses of particles in the system. It does not depend on the external force acting on the particles. Therefore, force acting on particle does not affect the centre of mass.
```
