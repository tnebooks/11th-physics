**A disc of moment of inertia I₁ is rotating in a horizontal plane about its symmetry axis with a constant angular speed ω. Another disc of moment of inertia I₂ is dropped coaxially on to the rotating disc. Then, both the discs rotate with same constant angular speed. The loss of kinetic energy due to friction in this process is, (AIUPMT 2001)**

```markdown
By conservation of angular momentum, I₁ω = (I₁ + I₂)ω', so ω' = I₁ω/(I₁ + I₂). Initial KE = ½I₁ω². Final KE = ½(I₁ + I₂)ω'² = ½I₁²ω²/(I₁ + I₂). Loss of KE = ½I₁ω² - ½I₁²ω²/(I₁ + I₂) = ½I₁I₂ω²/(I₁ + I₂).
```
