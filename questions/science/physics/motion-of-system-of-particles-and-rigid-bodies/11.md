**The ratio of the acceleration for a solid sphere (mass m and radius R) rolling down an incline of angle θ without slipping and slipping down the incline without rolling is, (AIUPMT 2014)**

```markdown
For rolling without slipping, a_rolling = g sinθ / (1 + I/mR²). For solid sphere, I = 2/5 mR², so a_rolling = g sinθ / (1 + 2/5) = 5/7 g sinθ. For slipping without rolling, a_slipping = g sinθ. Ratio a_rolling : a_slipping = 5/7 : 1 = 5:7.
```
