**From a disc of radius R and mass M, a circular hole of diameter R, whose rim passes through the centre is cut. What is the moment of inertia of the remaining part of the disc about a perpendicular axis passing through it? (NEET 2016)**

```markdown
Moment of inertia of full disc I_full = ½MR². Mass of removed portion = M/4. Its moment of inertia about its own centre = ½(M/4)(R/2)² = MR²/32. Using parallel axis theorem, I_removed = MR²/32 + (M/4)(R/2)² = MR²/32 + MR²/16 = 3MR²/32. I_remaining = I_full - I_removed = ½MR² - 3MR²/32 = 13MR²/32.
```
