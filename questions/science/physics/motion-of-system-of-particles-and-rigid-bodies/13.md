**The speed of a solid sphere after rolling down from rest without sliding on an inclined plane of vertical height h is, (AIUPMT 2014)**

```markdown
By conservation of energy, mgh = ½mv² + ½Iω². For solid sphere, I = 2/5 mR² and ω = v/R. So mgh = ½mv² + ½(2/5 mR²)(v²/R²) = ½mv² + 1/5 mv² = 7/10 mv². Therefore, v = sqrt(10gh/7).
```
