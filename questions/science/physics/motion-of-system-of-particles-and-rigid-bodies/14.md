**The speed of the centre of a wheel rolling on a horizontal surface is v₀. A point on the rim in level with the centre will be moving at a speed of, (PMT 1992, PMT 2003, IIT 2004)**

```markdown
For a rolling wheel, the velocity of a point on the rim is the vector sum of translational velocity v₀ and rotational velocity v₀ (perpendicular to radius). At a point level with the centre, these two velocities are perpendicular, so the resultant speed = sqrt(v₀² + v₀²) = √2 v₀.
```
