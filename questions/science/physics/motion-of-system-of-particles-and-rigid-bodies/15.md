**A round object of mass M and radius R rolls down without slipping along an inclined plane. The frictional force, (PMT 2005)**

```markdown
In rolling without slipping, static friction does no work as there is no relative motion at the point of contact. The frictional force provides the torque necessary for rotation and converts some translational energy into rotational energy.
```
