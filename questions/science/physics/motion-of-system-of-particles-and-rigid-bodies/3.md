**A particle is moving with a constant velocity along a line parallel to positive X-axis. The magnitude of its angular momentum with respect to the origin is, (IIT 2002)**

```markdown
Angular momentum L = mvr sinθ. For a particle moving along a line parallel to the X-axis, the perpendicular distance from the origin to the line of motion is constant. Therefore, the magnitude of angular momentum remains constant throughout the motion.
```
