choices:
  - L

  - 2L
answers:
  - L/√2

**A rigid body rotates with an angular momentum L. If its kinetic energy is halved, the angular momentum becomes, (AFMC 1998, AIPMT 2015)**

```markdown
Kinetic energy K = L²/2I. If K is halved, K' = K/2. Then L'²/2I = (L²/2I)/2 = L²/4I. Therefore, L'² = L²/2, so L' = L/√2.
```
