**A particle undergoes uniform circular motion. The angular momentum of the particle remains conserved about, (IIT 2003)**

```markdown
For a particle in uniform circular motion, the angular momentum is conserved about the centre of the circle because the centripetal force passes through the centre, producing zero torque. About any other point, torque is not zero.
```
