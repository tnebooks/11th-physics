**When a mass is rotating in a plane about a fixed point, its angular momentum is directed along, (AIPMT 2012)**

```markdown
Angular momentum L = r × p. The direction of L is perpendicular to the plane containing r and p, which is the plane of rotation. So L is directed along a line perpendicular to the plane of rotation.
```
