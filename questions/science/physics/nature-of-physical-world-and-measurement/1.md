**One of the combinations from the fundamental physical constants is $\dfrac{hc}{G}$. The unit of this expression is**

```markdown
The SI units are:

- Planck's constant, $h = \mathrm{kg\,m^2\,s^{-1}}$
- Speed of light, $c = \mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}$
- Gravitational constant, $G = \mathrm{m^3\,kg^{-1}\,s^{-2}}$

Hence,


<div class="math-display">$$
\frac{hc}{G}
=
\frac{(\mathrm{kg\,m^2\,s^{-1}})(\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}})}
{\mathrm{m^3\,kg^{-1}\,s^{-2}}}
=
\mathrm{kg^2}
$$</div>


Therefore, the unit of $\dfrac{hc}{G}$ is **kg²**.

**Correct Answer:** (a) kg²
```
