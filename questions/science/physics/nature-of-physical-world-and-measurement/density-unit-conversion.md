**The density of a material in CGS system of units is 4 g cm⁻³. In a system of units in which unit of length is 10 cm and unit of mass is 100 g, then the value of density of material will be**

```markdown
Density = 4 g cm⁻³.  
New unit of mass = 100 g → 1 g = 1/100 new mass unit.  
New unit of length = 10 cm → 1 cm = 1/10 new length unit.  

$4 \text{ g cm}^{-3} = 4 \frac{(1/100)}{(1/10)^3} = 4 \times \frac{1/100}{1/1000} = 4 \times 10 = 40$ new units.

Correct answer: (c) 40.
```
