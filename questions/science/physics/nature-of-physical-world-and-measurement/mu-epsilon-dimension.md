**The dimension of $(\mu_0 \varepsilon_0)^{-\frac{1}{2}}$ is [Main AIPMT 2011]**

```markdown
$\mu_0$ is permeability and $\varepsilon_0$ is permittivity.  
$\frac{1}{\sqrt{\mu_0 \varepsilon_0}}$ is the speed of light in vacuum, $c$.  

Therefore, $(\mu_0 \varepsilon_0)^{-\frac{1}{2}}$ has the dimension of velocity.

Correct answer: (c) velocity.
```
