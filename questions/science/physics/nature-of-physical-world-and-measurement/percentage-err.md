**The length of a body is measured as 3.51 m, if the accuracy is 0.01 m, then the percentage error in the measurement is**

```markdown
Percentage error = $\frac{\text{accuracy}}{\text{measured value}} \times 100 = \frac{0.01}{3.51} \times 100 \approx 0.28\%$. Correct answer: (c) 0.28%.
```
