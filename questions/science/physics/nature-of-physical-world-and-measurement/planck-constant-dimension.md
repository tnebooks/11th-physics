**The dimensional formula of Planck's constant $h$ is [JEE Main, NEET]**

```markdown
$E = h\nu \implies h = \frac{E}{\nu}$. Energy $E$ has dimensions $[ML^2T^{-2}]$ and frequency $\nu$ has dimensions $[T^{-1}]$. Therefore, $[h] = \frac{[ML^2T^{-2}]}{[T^{-1}]} = [ML^2T^{-1}]$. Correct answer: (a) $[ML^2T^{-1}]$.
```
