**If the force is proportional to square of velocity, then the dimension of proportionality constant is [JEE-2000]**

```markdown
$F \propto v^2 \implies F = k v^2 \implies k = \frac{F}{v^2}$.  
$[F] = [MLT^{-2}]$, $[v^2] = [L^2T^{-2}]$.  
$[k] = \frac{[MLT^{-2}]}{[L^2T^{-2}]} = [ML^{-1}T^0]$. Correct answer: (d) $[ML^{-1}T^0]$.
```
