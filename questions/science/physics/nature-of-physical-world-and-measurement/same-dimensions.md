**Which of the following pairs of physical quantities have same dimension?**

```markdown
- Force: $[MLT^{-2}]$
- Power: $[ML^2T^{-3}]$
- Torque: $[ML^2T^{-2}]$
- Energy: $[ML^2T^{-2}]$

Torque and energy have the same dimensions $[ML^2T^{-2}]$. Correct answer: (b) torque and energy.
```
