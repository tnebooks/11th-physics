**If the error in the measurement of radius is 2%, then the error in the determination of volume of the sphere will be**

```markdown
Volume of sphere $V = \frac{4}{3}\pi r^3$. Percentage error in $V$ is $3 \times$ (percentage error in $r$) = $3 \times 2\% = 6\%$. Correct answer: (d) 6%.
```
