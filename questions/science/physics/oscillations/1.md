**In a simple harmonic oscillation, the acceleration against displacement for one complete oscillation will be (model NSEP 2000-01)**

```markdown
For SHM, acceleration a = -ω²x. So the graph between acceleration and displacement is a straight line passing through the origin with negative slope.
```
