**A pendulum is hung in a very high building oscillates to and fro motion freely like a simple harmonic oscillator. If the acceleration of the bob is 16 m/s² at a distance of 4 m from the mean position, then the time period is (NEET 2018 model)**

```markdown
For SHM, acceleration a = -ω²x. Magnitude: |a| = ω²x. So 16 = ω² × 4, ω² = 4, ω = 2 rad/s. Time period T = 2π/ω = 2π/2 = π s.
```
