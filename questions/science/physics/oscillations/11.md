**A hollow sphere is filled with water. It is hung by a long thread. As the water flows out of a hole at the bottom, the period of oscillation will**

```markdown
As water flows out, the centre of mass of the sphere first moves down (increasing the effective length of the pendulum), so the time period increases. When the water level falls below the centre, the centre of mass moves up again, decreasing the effective length, so the time period decreases. Therefore, the period first increases and then decreases.
```
