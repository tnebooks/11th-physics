**Let the total energy of a particle executing simple harmonic motion with angular frequency is 1 rad/s is 0.256 J. If the displacement of the particle at time t = π/2 s is 8√2 cm then the amplitude of motion is**

```markdown
Total energy E = ½mω²A². Displacement y = A sin(ωt + φ). At t = π/2 s, y = 8√2 cm = 0.08√2 m. Let ω = 1 rad/s. y = A sin(π/2 + φ) = A cosφ. E = ½mω²A² = 0.256. Also, at t=0, from energy, if we assume φ=0, y = A sin(π/2) = A, so A = 0.08√2 m = 8√2 cm. Then E = ½m(1)²(0.08√2)² = 0.5m × 0.0128 = 0.0064m = 0.256, so m = 40 kg.
```
