**A simple pendulum is suspended from the roof of a school bus which moves in a horizontal direction with an acceleration a, then the time period is**

```markdown
When the pendulum is in an accelerated frame, the effective acceleration g_eff = √(g² + a²). The time period is T = 2π√(l/g_eff) = 2π√(l/√(g² + a²)). The correct option is T = √(l/√(g² + a²)).
```
