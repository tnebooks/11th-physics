**The displacement of a simple harmonic motion is given by y(t) = A sin(ωt + φ). Let the amplitude of the oscillation be 8 cm and the time period of the oscillation is 24 s. If the displacement at initial time (t = 0 s) is 4 cm, then the displacement at t = 6 s is**

```markdown
T = 24 s, ω = 2π/T = π/12 rad/s. At t = 0, y = 4 = 8 sinφ, so sinφ = 1/2, φ = π/6. At t = 6, y = 8 sin(π/12 × 6 + π/6) = 8 sin(π/2 + π/6) = 8 sin(2π/3) = 8 × √3/2 = 4√3 cm.
```
