**A simple pendulum has a time period T₁. When its point of suspension is moved vertically upwards according as y = kt², where y is vertical distance covered and k = 1 m/s², its time period becomes T₂. Then T₂/T₁ is (g = 10 m/s²) (IIT 2005)**
