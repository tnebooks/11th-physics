**Consider two wires X and Y. The radius of wire X is 3 times the radius of Y. If they are stretched by the same load then the stress on Y is**

```markdown
Stress = Force/Area. Area is proportional to r². If r_X = 3r_Y, then A_X = 9A_Y. For the same force, stress_Y = F/A_Y = F/(A_X/9) = 9F/A_X = 9 × stress_X. Therefore, stress on Y is nine times that on X.
```
