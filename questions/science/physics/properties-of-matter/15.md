**In a horizontal pipe of non-uniform cross section, water flows with a velocity of 1 m/s at a point where the diameter of the pipe is 20 cm. The velocity of water at a point where the diameter of the pipe is (in cm) is 1.5 m/s**

```markdown
By equation of continuity, A₁v₁ = A₂v₂. A = πd²/4. So d₁²v₁ = d₂²v₂. Given d₁ = 20 cm, v₁ = 1 m/s, v₂ = 1.5 m/s. (20)² × 1 = d₂² × 1.5. d₂² = 400/1.5 ≈ 266.67. d₂ ≈ 16.3 cm, which is approximately 16 cm.
```
