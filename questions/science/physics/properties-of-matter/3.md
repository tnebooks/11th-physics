**The load-elongation graph of three wires of the same material are shown in figure. Which of the following wire is the thickest?**

![](img.png)

```markdown
For wires of the same material, Young's modulus Y is constant. Y = (F/A)/(ΔL/L). For a given load F and elongation ΔL, the wire with larger area A (thicker wire) will have less elongation. The graph with the smallest slope (least elongation for a given load) corresponds to the thickest wire.
```
