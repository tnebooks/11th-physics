**For a given material, the rigidity modulus is 1/3 of Young's modulus. Its Poisson's ratio is**

```markdown
Relation between Y, G and σ: Y = 2G(1 + σ). Given G = Y/3. Substituting: Y = 2(Y/3)(1 + σ) = (2Y/3)(1 + σ). Dividing by Y: 1 = (2/3)(1 + σ), so 1 + σ = 3/2, therefore σ = 1/2 = 0.5.
```
