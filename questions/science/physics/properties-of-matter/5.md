**A small sphere of radius r falls from rest in a viscous liquid. Heat is produced due to viscous force. The rate of production of heat when the sphere attains its terminal velocity is proportional to (NEET model 2018)**

```markdown
Terminal velocity v ∝ r². Viscous force F = 6πηrv ∝ r × r² = r³. Rate of heat production = power = F × v ∝ r³ × r² = r⁵.
```
