**With an increase in temperature, the viscosity of liquid and gas, respectively will**

```markdown
For liquids, viscosity decreases with increase in temperature because intermolecular forces weaken. For gases, viscosity increases with increase in temperature because molecular collisions increase. Therefore, liquid decreases and gas increases.
```
