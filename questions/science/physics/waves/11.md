**Which of the following represents a wave?**

```markdown
A wave function must be of the form f(x ± vt). Among the options, sin(x + vt) represents a wave traveling in the negative x direction. (x - vt)², x(x + vt), and 1/(x + vt) do not represent standard wave forms.
```
