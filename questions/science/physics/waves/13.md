**Let y = 1/(1 + x²) at t = 0 s be the amplitude of the wave propagating in the positive x-direction. At t = 2s, the amplitude of the wave propagating becomes y = 1/(1 + (x - 2)²). Assume that the shape of the wave does not change during propagation. The velocity of the wave is**

```markdown
The wave has shifted by 2 m in 2 seconds. So velocity v = displacement/time = 2/2 = 1 m/s.
```
