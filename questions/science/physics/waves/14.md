**A uniform rope having mass m hangs vertically from a rigid support. A transverse wave pulse is produced at the lower end. Which of the following plots shows the correct variation of speed v with height h from the lower end?**

![](img1.png)

```markdown
Speed of wave on a string v = √(T/μ). Tension T at height h from lower end equals weight of rope below that point = μgh (where μ = mass per unit length). So v = √(μgh/μ) = √(gh). So v ∝ √h, which is a parabolic curve starting from zero at the lower end. The correct graph is option (d).
```
