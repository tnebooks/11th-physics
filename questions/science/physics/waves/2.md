**A transverse wave moves from a medium A to a medium B. In medium A, the velocity of the transverse wave is 500 m/s and the wavelength is 5 m. The frequency and the wavelength of the wave in medium B when its velocity is 600 m/s, respectively are**

```markdown
Frequency remains constant when wave moves from one medium to another. f = v/λ = 500/5 = 100 Hz. In medium B, λ = v/f = 600/100 = 6 m. So frequency = 100 Hz and wavelength = 6 m.
```
