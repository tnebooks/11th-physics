**For a particular tube, among six harmonic frequencies below 1000 Hz, only four harmonic frequencies are given: 300 Hz, 600 Hz, 750 Hz and 900 Hz. What are the two other frequencies missing from this list?**

```markdown
The harmonics suggest the fundamental frequency divides all given frequencies. The common difference between consecutive harmonics should be constant. The sequence could be 150, 300, 450, 600, 750, 900. The missing frequencies are 150 Hz and 450 Hz.
```
