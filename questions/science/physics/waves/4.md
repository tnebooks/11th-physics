**Which of the following options is correct? (1) Quality, (2) Pitch, (3) Loudness**

![](img.png)

```markdown
Quality corresponds to Waveform (B). Pitch corresponds to Frequency (C). Loudness corresponds to Intensity (A). So the correct order is (B), (C) and (A).
```
