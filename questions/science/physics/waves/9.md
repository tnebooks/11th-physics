**The displacement y of a wave travelling in the x direction is given by y = (2 × 10⁻³) sin(300t - 2x + π/4), where x and y are measured in metres and t in second. The speed of the wave is**

```markdown
Comparing with y = A sin(ωt - kx), we get ω = 300 rad/s, k = 2 rad/m. Velocity v = ω/k = 300/2 = 150 m/s.
```
