**A uniform force of $(2\hat{i} + \hat{j}) \, \text{N}$ acts on a particle of mass 1 kg. The particle displaces from position $(3\hat{j} + \hat{k}) \, \text{m}$ to $(5\hat{i} + 3\hat{j}) \, \text{m}$. The work done by the force on the particle is**

```markdown
Work done W = F · d, where d is the displacement vector. Displacement d = (5i + 3j) - (3j + k) = 5i - k. Force F = 2i + j. Therefore, W = (2i + j) · (5i - k) = (2)(5) + (1)(0) + (0)(-1) = 10 J.
```
