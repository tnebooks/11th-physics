---
choices:
  - $F = \frac{\beta}{2} x^2$
  - $F = -\beta x$
  - $F = -\frac{\beta}{2} x^2$
answers:
  - $F = \beta x$
---

**If the potential energy of the particle is U = α - (β/2)x², then force experienced by the particle is**

```markdown
Force F = -dU/dx. U = α - (β/2)x². dU/dx = -βx. Therefore, F = -(-βx) = βx.
```
