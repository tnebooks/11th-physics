**A particle which is constrained to move along x-axis, is subjected to a force in the same direction which varies with the distance x of the particle from the origin as F(x) = -kx + ax³. Here, k and a are positive constants. For x ≥ 0, the functional form of the potential energy U(x) of the particle is (IIIT 2002)**

![](img1.png)

```markdown
Force F = -dU/dx. Given F = -kx + ax³, so -dU/dx = -kx + ax³, which gives dU/dx = kx - ax³. Integrating, U = ½kx² - ¼ax⁴ + C. Taking U(0) = 0, C = 0. So U = ½kx² - ¼ax⁴. This corresponds to option (d).
```
