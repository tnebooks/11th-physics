**A spring of force constant k is cut into two pieces such that one piece is double the length of the other. Then, the long piece will have a force constant of**

```markdown
Spring constant k is inversely proportional to length. If total length is L, the long piece has length 2L/3 and short piece has length L/3. For the long piece, k_long = k × (L)/(2L/3) = 3k/2.
```
