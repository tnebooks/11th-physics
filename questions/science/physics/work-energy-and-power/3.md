**A body of mass 1 kg is thrown upwards with a velocity 20 m/s. It momentarily comes to rest after attaining a height of 18 m. How much energy is lost due to air friction? (Take g = 10 m/s²)**

```markdown
Initial kinetic energy = ½ × 1 × (20)² = 200 J. Final potential energy at height 18 m = mgh = 1 × 10 × 18 = 180 J. Energy lost due to air friction = Initial KE - Final PE = 200 - 180 = 20 J.
```
