**A body of mass 4m is lying in xy-plane at rest. It suddenly explodes into three pieces. Two pieces each of mass m move perpendicular to each other with equal speed v. The total kinetic energy generated due to explosion is (AIPMT 2014)**

```markdown
The two pieces of mass m each have kinetic energy = ½mv². Total KE of two pieces = mv². By conservation of momentum, the third piece of mass 2m moves with velocity v/√2. Its KE = ½ × 2m × (v/√2)² = ½ × 2m × v²/2 = ½mv². Total KE = mv² + ½mv² = 3/2 mv².
```
