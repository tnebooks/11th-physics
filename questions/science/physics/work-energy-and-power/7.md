**What is the minimum velocity with which a body of mass m must enter a vertical loop of radius R so that it can complete the loop?**

```markdown
For a body to complete a vertical loop, the minimum velocity at the lowest point is sqrt(5gR). At the top, the condition is v_top = sqrt(gR). Using conservation of energy, ½mv² = ½m(gR) + mg(2R), which gives v = sqrt(5gR).
```
